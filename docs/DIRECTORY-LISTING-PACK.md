## 2. Descriptions — four lengths

Most directories ask for one of these. Pick the one that fits the character limit and change nothing.

### Short (under 90 characters)
```
CRM, field workforce and sales & distribution in one platform, powered by WhatsApp and AI.
```

### Medium (under 160 characters)
```
OZZO unifies CRM, field workforce tracking and sales & distribution in one platform — a web dashboard for managers and an Android app for field reps.
```

### Long (under 400 characters)
```
OZZO is an all-in-one CRM, field workforce and sales & distribution platform built for Indian SMEs and MSMEs. Managers get a web dashboard; field reps get an Android app that works offline. Capture leads, run WhatsApp conversations from a shared inbox with an AI assistant, track selfie + GPS attendance and geo-tagged visits, plan beat routes, take orders offline, and collect payments with outstanding that calculates itself.
```

### Full (no limit)
```
OZZO is a business software platform that brings CRM, field workforce management and sales & distribution into one system, built specifically for Indian SMEs and MSMEs.

There are two products. OZZO CRM is the front office: leads, deals and visual Kanban pipelines, a shared WhatsApp team inbox with an AI assistant that answers routine questions from your own knowledge base, branded PDF quotations, and one customer record everyone works from.

OZZO SFA is the field: selfie and GPS attendance, live location, geo-tagged visits, beat routes and territory management, offline order capture, dispatch, field payment collection with self-calculating outstanding, automatic stock, trade schemes, customer-specific price lists, and distributor/dealer/retailer trade levels.

Managers work from a web dashboard. Field reps use an Android app that works offline and syncs when signal returns. Reporting includes sales analytics, ageing and a per-rep Daily Sales Report.

OZZO is used across FMCG, agri-inputs, pharma, building materials, paints, pipes, plywood, batteries, lubricants, auto parts and other industries where a team sells or services on a beat.
```

## 5. The five India directories — in this order

Do them one at a time. Each takes about 20 minutes.

### 1. SoftwareSuggest — biggest for Indian B2B software
- Go to **softwaresuggest.com** → *Get Listed* / *Vendor Login*
- Register with `hello@ozzo.co.in`
- Category: **Sales Force Automation Software**, then also add *Field Sales* and *CRM*
- Paste the **Long** description
- Add the feature list, pricing, screenshots
- They usually call to verify. Expect a sales pitch for paid placement — the free listing is enough for now.

### 2. Techjockey
- **techjockey.com** → *Sell on Techjockey* / vendor signup
- Same category and description
- They ask for a product demo video — skip it if you don't have one, the listing still publishes

### 3. Capterra India (also gets you GetApp + Software Advice)
- **capterra.in** → scroll to *Vendors* → *List your software*
- One Gartner Digital Markets account covers Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice — **three listings from one form**
- Category: **Field Sales Software**
- This is the highest-value one: Capterra already ranks #1 for your target queries

### 4. GoodFirms
- **goodfirms.co** → *Get Listed*
- Needs a company profile plus the product
- Fill the "Services" and "Clients" sections — empty sections rank worse

### 5. SaaSworthy
- **saasworthy.com** → *List your product*
- Quickest of the five
- Category: **Sales Force Automation**
