Links & socials

Toggle any row off to hide it. Add Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Telegram, a maps pin, or a custom link — paste the full URL.
To publish your changes:
1. Click Download updated card.html.
2. Open your repo → github.com/sumitvegad07-alt/ozzo-website → the public folder → click card.html → the pencil (Edit) → delete all, paste the new file… or use Add file → Upload files and drop the downloaded card.html to replace it.
3. Commit. It's live at ozzo.co.in/card in ~1 minute. The link & QR never change.
